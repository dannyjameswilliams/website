I did my PhD at the University of Bristol as part of the COMPASS CDT, supervised by Song Liu, on
truncated and unnormalised probability density estimation. I since have been working as a Machine 
Learning Engineer in applied AI, creating demo applications, internal tools and developing the backend
for the Query Agent, Weaviate's paid agentic RAG service.

Before that I studied Mathematics at the University of Exeter, where my Masters dissertation used
extreme value theory to model rainfall extremes across the South West of England.

Everything else here is work that I am proud of. Whether it is the open-source [agentic RAG application, Elysia](/projects/elysia) 
I developed for Weaviate, or the by-product of curiosity: what BERT thinks the difference is between
[Eddard and Bran Stark](/blog/asoiaf/) in A Game of Thrones, whether
[people can pick a random number](/blog/randomchoices/), and my horribly outdated
[investigation into the lyrics of Kanye West via old-school NLP](/blog/kanye/) from 2020.

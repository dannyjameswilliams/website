Developed a data-driven solution to truncated probability density estimation by solving a
constrained optimisation problem and minimising a modified kernelised Stein discrepancy. I showed
theoretical guarantees for this estimator and ran numerical experiments demonstrating improvements
over state-of-the-art methods.

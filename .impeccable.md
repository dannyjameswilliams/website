# Design Context

### Users

Collaborators, hiring managers at AI labs, fellow researchers, and readers who arrived from a paper
or a link to a blog post. They read on a laptop at a desk, in daylight, and they are here to answer
one of three questions quickly: *what has he published*, *what has he built*, *can he write*.

The site is a reading surface, not a showcase. Long-form technical prose with mathematics is the
primary content type.

### Brand Personality

**Precise. Ink-on-paper. Quietly opinionated.**

A well-set academic monograph, not a portfolio site. Confidence comes from the quality of the
typesetting and the substance of the content, never from effects. No persuasion, no marketing voice,
no exclamation marks.

### Aesthetic Direction

Derived directly from Danny's CV, which he likes and which is the design's north star:

- Alternating-weight wordmark: **Danny** *James* **Williams**
- Sparse forest-green accent, used only for headings, rules and marks
- Deep blue-slate ink, never black
- Asymmetric two-column structure: a narrow metadata rail beside a wide content column
- Justified body copy with automatic hyphenation, like a printed document
- Thin 1px rules as the only divider; no shadows, no card grids

**Theme:** light by default (it is a reading surface consumed in daylight), with a properly designed
dark mode for night reading — warm slate, not inverted, not black.

**Anti-references:** the generic dev-portfolio (dark hero, gradient text, glassmorphism, tech-stack
badge grids, identical three-up cards, monospace-as-personality).

### Design Principles

1. **The document is the design.** Every visual decision should be defensible as good typesetting.
2. **The accent is rare.** Green earns its power by appearing perhaps six times per screen.
3. **Structure over decoration.** Hierarchy comes from measure, weight and space, not containers.
4. **Content lives in markdown.** Anything Danny might want to change must be editable without
   touching a template.
5. **No ornament that a printer could not set.** If it needs a shadow or a blur to work, redesign it.
